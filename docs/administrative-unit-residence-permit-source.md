# Na upravni enoti – Dovoljenje za prebivanje in delo

**At the Administrative Unit – Residence and Work Permit**

> Source reference: a bilingual (Slovenian/English) practice dialogue for the scenario of
> submitting a residence-and-work-permit application at a Slovenian administrative unit
> (*upravna enota*). Reproduced in full for provenance; use as source material when
> authoring the corresponding rehearsal-dialogue scenario.

---

## Dialogue

**Uslužbenka:** Dober dan. Kako vam lahko pomagam?
**Clerk:** Good morning. How can I help you?

**Tujec:** Dober dan. Rad bi oddal vlogo za dovoljenje za prebivanje in delo.
**Foreigner:** Good morning. I would like to submit an application for a residence and work permit.

**Uslužbenka:** Ste naročeni?
**Clerk:** Do you have an appointment?

**Tujec:** Da, termin imam ob deveti uri.
**Foreigner:** Yes, I have an appointment at nine o'clock.

**Uslužbenka:** Prosim za vaš potni list.
**Clerk:** May I have your passport, please?

**Tujec:** Izvolite.
**Foreigner:** Here you are.

**Uslužbenka:** Ali imate vse potrebne dokumente?
**Clerk:** Do you have all the required documents?

**Tujec:** Mislim, da jih imam. Tukaj so pogodba o zaposlitvi, fotografija in dokazilo o zdravstvenem zavarovanju.
**Foreigner:** I think so. Here are my employment contract, a photograph, and proof of health insurance.

**Uslužbenka:** Ali imate tudi izpolnjen obrazec?
**Clerk:** Do you also have the completed application form?

**Tujec:** Da, tukaj je.
**Foreigner:** Yes, here it is.

**Uslužbenka:** Samo trenutek, prosim. Preverila bom dokumente.
**Clerk:** One moment, please. I'll check your documents.

*(Po nekaj minutah.)*
*(A few minutes later.)*

**Uslužbenka:** Dokumenti so skoraj popolni, vendar manjka potrdilo o prijavi prebivališča.
**Clerk:** The documents are almost complete, but you're missing proof of residence registration.

**Tujec:** Kje ga lahko dobim?
**Foreigner:** Where can I get it?

**Uslužbenka:** Če že živite v Sloveniji, ga lahko uredite tukaj.
**Clerk:** If you already live in Slovenia, you can arrange it here.

**Tujec:** Imam najemno pogodbo. Ali je to dovolj?
**Foreigner:** I have a rental agreement. Is that enough?

**Uslužbenka:** Da, to bo v redu.
**Clerk:** Yes, that will be fine.

**Tujec:** Koliko časa traja postopek?
**Foreigner:** How long does the procedure take?

**Uslužbenka:** Običajno približno en mesec.
**Clerk:** Usually about one month.

**Tujec:** Kako bom izvedel, da je dovoljenje pripravljeno?
**Foreigner:** How will I know when the permit is ready?

**Uslužbenka:** Poslali vam bomo obvestilo po pošti ali po elektronski pošti.
**Clerk:** We will send you a notification by mail or email.

**Tujec:** Koliko znaša upravna taksa?
**Foreigner:** How much is the administrative fee?

**Uslužbenka:** Sedemdeset evrov.
**Clerk:** Seventy euros.

**Tujec:** Ali lahko plačam s kartico?
**Foreigner:** Can I pay by card?

**Uslužbenka:** Seveda.
**Clerk:** Of course.

**Tujec:** Hvala za pomoč. Nasvidenje.
**Foreigner:** Thank you for your help. Goodbye.

**Uslužbenka:** Nasvidenje in lep dan.
**Clerk:** Goodbye, and have a nice day.
