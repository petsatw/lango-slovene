## 💡 Ideas

> Dump raw ideas here.
> 

---

🗣️ **Pronunciation coach**
AI listens to your pronunciation and gives real-time feedback on Slovenian vowel sounds and stress patterns, which are tricky for English speakers. Could use Whisper for transcription + Claude for feedback.

---

🧩 **Contextual flashcards**
Instead of isolated words, show vocabulary inside full Slovenian sentences with cultural context — e.g. Ljubljana everyday speech vs. formal written Slovenian.

---

🗺️ **Dialect explorer**
Slovenian has 7 major dialect groups (Gorenjska, Dolenjska, Štajerska, etc.). Let users explore how the same phrase sounds across regions via audio samples.

---

🤖 **AI conversation partner**
Practice real dialogue with an AI tutor that corrects grammar inline without breaking the flow of conversation. Could offer themed scenarios (at the market, at a café, giving directions).

---

📖 **Story-based learning**
Short Slovenian stories with difficulty levels. Users read, tap unknown words for instant AI-powered explanations, and unlock the next chapter as vocabulary grows.

---

🎮 **Gamified grammar challenges**
Daily mini-games focused on Slovenian's 6 grammatical cases (nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, locative, instrumental) — a known pain point for learners.

## ⚡ Features

### 🟢 MVP — Must Have

- [ ]  **User onboarding & level assessment** — Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced self-placement quiz
- [ ]  **Daily lesson with AI-generated exercises** — Grammar, vocabulary, listening comprehension
- [ ]  **AI chat tutor (text)** — Free-form conversation practice with inline corrections
- [ ]  **Vocabulary bank** — Personal dictionary that grows as the user learns
- [ ]  **Progress tracking dashboard** — Streaks, vocabulary count, accuracy over time
- [ ]  **Basic audio playback** — Native speaker recordings for new words and phrases

### 🟡 v2 — Nice to Have

- [ ]  **Speech recognition & pronunciation scoring** — Whisper API integration with phoneme-level feedback
- [ ]  **Dialect audio samples** — Regional Slovenian variants (Gorenjska, Štajerska, etc.)
- [ ]  **Spaced repetition flashcard system** — SM-2 algorithm or similar for vocabulary retention
- [ ]  **Grammar case drill mode** — Focused practice on Slovenian's 6 cases
- [ ]  **Themed conversation scenarios** — AI roleplay (café, travel, work, family)
- [ ]  **Offline mode (basic)** — Downloaded lesson packs for offline use

### 🔵 Future / Explore

- [ ]  **Community forums & leaderboards** — Social learning, peer challenges
- [ ]  **Gamification system** — XP, badges, level-up moments
- [ ]  **Media immersion mode** — RTV Slovenia / YouTube integration with AI subtitles
- [ ]  **Teacher dashboard** — For Slovenian language tutors to assign and track student progress
- [ ]  **Dialect explorer** — Interactive map with audio samples by region
- [ ]  **Story-based reading module** — Graded Slovenian fiction with tap-to-translate
