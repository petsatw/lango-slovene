# Obisk knjižnice — eno drevo, vse proti desni
## A visit to the library — everything flows rightward

> Cel pogovor, od pozdrava do vsake replike, teče v eno smer — proti desni.
> *(The whole conversation, from greeting to every line, flows one way — to the right.)*

## 1. Delovni čas knjižnice — Library opening hours

**1.1 Delovni čas / Opening hours** — `A2`

- **O:** Ali mi lahko, prosim, poveste, kakšen imate delovni čas?
  *"Could you please tell me your opening hours?"*
- **K:** Od ponedeljka do petka smo odprti od 8. do 19. ure, ob sobotah pa od 8. do 13. ure.
  *"Monday to Friday we're open from 8 to 19, and on Saturdays from 8 to 13."*

  ⏳ *complication branch:*
  - **O:** Kaj pa med poletnimi počitnicami? Velja enak urnik?
    *"What about during the summer holidays? Is it the same schedule?"*
  - **K:** Poleti imamo prilagojen urnik – ob sredah smo odprti popoldne, ostale dni pa dopoldne. Točen urnik imamo vedno objavljen tudi na naši spletni strani.
    *"In summer we have an adjusted schedule – Wednesdays we're open in the afternoon, other days in the morning. The exact schedule is always posted on our website too."*

    ✅ *positive close:*
    - **O:** Odlično, hvala za informacijo!
      *"Great, thanks for the information!"*
    - **K:** Ni za kaj, lepo se imejte!
      *"You're welcome, have a nice day!"*

## 2. Vpis in članstvo — Registration & membership

**2.1 Prvič se vpisati / First-time registration** — `A2`

- **O:** Rad/a bi se vpisal/a v knjižnico. — *"I would like to register at the library."*
- **K:** Seveda. Ali imate s sabo osebni dokument? — *"Of course. Do you have your ID with you?"*

  ↳ **Branch — do you have ID?**

  - ✅ *positive:*
    - **O:** Ja, tukaj imate. — *"Yes, here you are."*
    - **K:** Odlično, vse je v redu. Tukaj je vaša nova izkaznica. Dobrodošli!
      *"Excellent, all in order. Here is your new card. Welcome!"*

  - ⚠️ *problem — no ID:*
    - **O:** Žal ga nimam pri sebi. — *"Unfortunately I don't have it with me."*
    - **K:** Žal vas brez osebnega dokumenta ne morem vpisati. Ko boste imeli dokument s seboj, se vrnite.
      *"I'm sorry, but I can't register you without an ID. Please come back when you have it with you."*

**2.2 Izkaznica ti je potekla / Renewing an expired card** — `B1`

- **O:** Izkaznica mi je potekla. — *"My card has expired."*
- **K:** Preverimo... res je, želite podaljšati? — *"Let's check... indeed it has, would you like to renew it?"*

  - ✅ *positive:*
    - **O:** Da, prosim. — *"Yes, please."*
    - **K:** Samo potrdite mi vaš trenutni naslov, prosim. — *"Just confirm your current address for me, please."*
      - **O:** Naslov je enak kot prej. — *"Same as before."*
      - **K:** Odlično, izkaznica je podaljšana za eno leto. — *"Excellent, the card is renewed for one year."*

  - ⚠️ *problem — unpaid fine:*
    - **K:** Imate neporavnano zamudnino, za podaljšanje jo morate najprej poravnati.
      *"You have an unpaid fine — you'll need to settle it before renewing."*
    - **O:** Seveda, koliko dolgujem? — *"Of course, how much do I owe?"*
    - **K:** Zamudnina znaša 2 €. Ko poravnate, vam bomo podaljšali članstvo!
      *"The fine is €2. Once paid, we'll renew your membership!"*

**2.3 Izgubiti izkaznico / Lost library card** — `A2`

- **O:** Izgubil/a sem izkaznico, kaj naj naredim? — *"I lost my card. What should I do?"*
- **K:** Ni problema, izdamo vam novo. Taksa je okoli 2 evra. — *"No problem, we'll issue you a new one. The fee is about 2 euros."*
- **K:** Hvala, tukaj je vaša nova izkaznica. Stara je zdaj neveljavna.
  *"Here is your new card. The old one is now invalid."*

## 3. Izposoja — Borrowing

**3.1 Standardna izposoja / Standard borrowing** — `A1`

- **O:** Rad/a bi si izposodil/a to knjigo. — *"I'd like to borrow this book."*
- **K:** Seveda, prosim izkaznico. — *"Sure, your card please."*

  - ✅ *positive:*
    - **K:** Izvolite. Rok vračila knjige je 30 dni. Prijetno branje!
      *"Here you go. The book is due back in 30 days. Enjoy your reading!"*

  - ⏳ *complication — not available:*
    - **K:** Žal knjiga trenutno ni na voljo za izposojo.
      *"Unfortunately the book is not currently available for borrowing."*

    - ⏳ *sub-branch — reserve for later:*
      - **K:** Na voljo bo čez 15 dni. Želite, da vam jo rezerviram?
        *"It will be available in 15 days. Would you like me to reserve it for you?"*
        - **O:** Da, prosim. — *"Yes, please."*
        - **O:** Ne, hvala. — *"No, thank you."*

    - ⏳ *sub-branch — reading room only:*
      - **K:** Ta izvod ni na voljo za izposojo na dom, lahko pa jo rezervirate za branje v čitalnici.
        *"This copy is not available for home borrowing, but you can reserve it to read in the reading room."*
        - **O:** Da, prosim. — *"Yes, please."*
        - **O:** Ne, hvala. — *"No, thank you."*

**3.2 Prevzem rezervirane knjige / Picking up a reserved book** — `A2`

- **O:** Rezerviral/a sem knjigo prek spleta, prišel/prišla sem jo prevzeti.
  *"I reserved a book online, I've come to pick it up."*
- **K:** Seveda, kako vam je ime, prosim? — *"Of course, what is your name, please?"*
- **O:** Slavko. — *"Slavko."*
- **K:** Trenutek, preverim... da, tukaj je. Prosim še za vašo izkaznico, da uredim izposojo.
  *"One moment, I'll check... yes, here it is. I also need your card, please, to arrange the borrowing."*
- **K:** Rok vračila knjige je 30 dni. Prijetno branje!
  *"The book's return deadline is 30 days. Enjoy your reading!"*

## 4. Vračanje — Returning items

**4.1 Pravočasno vračanje / Returned on time** — `A1`

- **O:** Rad/a bi vrnil/a to knjigo. — *"I'd like to return this book."*
- **K:** Hvala, vse je v redu. — *"Thanks, all good."*

**4.2 Zamujen rok / Returned late — pay now or later** — `B1`

- **O:** Rad/a bi vrnil/a to knjigo. — *"I'd like to return this book."*
- **K:** Vidim, da je rok za vračilo potekel pred petimi dnevi. — *"I see this book was overdue for five days."*
- **O:** Oprostite, pozabil/a sem. — *"Sorry, I forgot."*
- **K:** Ni problema, zamudnina znaša [znesek]. — *"No problem, the fine is [amount]."*

  ↳ **Branch — pay now or later?**

  - ✅ *positive — pay now:*
    - **O:** Plačam takoj. — *"I'll pay now."*
    - **K:** Hvala, tukaj je potrdilo. — *"Thanks, here's your receipt."*

  - ⚠️ *problem — pay later:*
    - **O:** Lahko plačam naslednjič? — *"Can I pay next time?"*
    - **K:** Da, a do plačila vam bo onemogočena nadaljnja izposoja.
      *"Yes, but borrowing is blocked till you pay."*

**4.3 Poškodovana / izgubljena / Damaged or lost book** — `B2`

- **O:** Poškodoval/a sem knjigo. — *"I damaged the book."*
- **K:** Poglejmo... škoda je precejšnja, obravnavamo jo kot izgubljeno.
  *"Let's see... the damage is considerable, we'll treat it as lost."*
- ⚠️ **K:** Plačati morate nadomestilo, ali prinesti enak izvod.
  *"Pay a replacement fee, or bring an identical copy."*

## 5. Uporaba prostora — Using the space

**5.1 Čitalnica / Reading room** — `A2`

- **O:** Rada bi se prijavila v čitalnico. — *"I would like to sign in to the reading room."*
- **K:** Seveda. Prosim, pokažite mi člansko izkaznico in izberite sedež.
  *"Of course. Please show me your library card and choose a seat."*
- **O:** Sedež B2, prosim. — *"Seat B2, please."*
- **K:** Sedež B2 je rezerviran. Ob vstopu se prijavite z izkaznico pri vhodu.
  *"Seat B2 is reserved. When you enter, check in with your card at the entrance."*

**5.2 Računalniki / Computers & internet** — `A1`

- **O:** Ali lahko uporabljam računalnik? — *"Can I use a computer?"*
- **K:** Seveda. Računalniki so v prvem nadstropju. — *"Of course. The computers are on the first floor."*
- **O:** Ali ga moram rezervirati? — *"Do I need to reserve it?"*

  ↳ **Branch — reservation needed?**

  - ✅ *positive — no reservation:*
    - **K:** Ne. Rezervacija ni potrebna. Če je kakšen prost, ga lahko uporabite.
      *"No. No reservation is needed. If one is free, you can use it."*

  - ⚠️ *complication — reservation required:*
    - **K:** Da, rezervacija je potrebna. Za rezervacijo potrebujem vašo člansko izkaznico.
      *"Yes, a reservation is required. I'll need your library card to reserve it."*

## 6. Drugo — Other situations

**6.1 Fotokopiranje / tiskanje / Copying & printing** — `A2`

- **O:** Ali lahko tukaj natisnem dokument? — *"Can I print a document here?"*
- **K:** Seveda! Pri vhodu imamo avtomat Printbox. — *"Of course! We have a Printbox machine by the entrance."*
- **O:** Kako pa to deluje? — *"How does that work?"*
- **K:** Dokument lahko naložite prek USB-ključka, e-pošte ali spletne strani Printbox. Na avtomatu se nato le prijavite in natisnete.
  *"You can upload the document via USB stick, email, or the Printbox website. Then just log in at the machine and print."*

  ✅ *positive close:*
  - **O:** Aha, razumem. Hvala, bom poskusil/a. — *"Ah, I understand. Thanks, I'll give it a try."*
  - **K:** Z veseljem! Če boste potrebovali pomoč, me kar pokličite.
    *"My pleasure! If you need help, just call me."*

**6.2 WiFi / Wireless internet** — `A1`

- **O:** Ali imate brezplačen WiFi? — *"Do you have free WiFi?"*
- **K:** Da, seveda. — *"Yes, of course."*
- **O:** Kakšno je geslo? — *"What is the password?"*
- **K:** Geslo je napisano na nalepki pri vhodu, ali pa vam ga povem: [geslo].
  *"The password is written on a sticker at the entrance, or I can tell you: [password]."*

  ✅ *positive close:*
  - **O:** Hvala lepa. — *"Thank you very much."*
  - **K:** Ni za kaj, prijetno delo! — *"You're welcome, enjoy your work!"*
