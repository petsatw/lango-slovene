Vadi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who runs Vadi

Vadi is run by Chris Prudhomme, Ljubljana, Slovenia (info@chrismakesiteasy.com). This policy covers Vadi at https://lango-slovene-production.up.railway.app/.

2. What Vadi keeps and why

  • An invite code, which links each learner's data.
  • Learning progress and details that the tutor needs to address you in the correct grammatical forms, such as gender.
  • The learning history: text transcripts of tutor sessions.
  • Basic usage and feedback records, and server logs.

This data is processed to provide the requested service, and, for usage records and logs, on the basis of legitimate interests in running and improving Vadi. The browser stores only the invite code and settings.

3. The live tutor

Transcripts are under the learner's control. Each tutor session is saved as a text transcript in the learning history. Any session, or all sessions, can be deleted in Settings at any time.

"Don't save this session" is available before every session. With it, the transcript is deleted when the session ends; if scoring fails, no progress is recorded and the transcript is still deleted.

Use of saved conversations to improve Vadi is optional and off by default, and can be withdrawn at any time. These copies have names removed and are kept for up to 90 days.

Voice is streamed live to Google (Gemini) or xAI (Grok) so the tutor can hear and answer, and is never stored by Vadi. ElevenLabs voices the tutor's replies.

Information shared during a live tutor session is the responsibility of the person sharing it. It is sent to Google or xAI for real-time processing and is subject to their terms and privacy policies: Google (Privacy Policy, Gemini API terms) and xAI (Privacy Policy).

4. Where the data goes

Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok) and ElevenLabs process data for the tutor, and Railway hosts the app.

Transfers to the United States are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

5. How long

Data is kept until 12 months after last use, or until deleted. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

6. Rights

Deletion is available in Settings, or by email to info@chrismakesiteasy.com with the invite code. Access, correction, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent are available the same way. Complaints can be lodged with the Slovenian Information Commissioner, Informacijski pooblaščenec (www.ip-rs.si).

7. Age

Vadi is intended for people aged 18 or over.

8. Changes

Changes are published on this page with a new effective date. Changes affecting voice or conversations are also shown on the tutor start screen.
